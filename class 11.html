Climate Change and Environmental Awarness

Introduction

Climate change is one of the biggest challenges facing the world today. Rising temperatures, melting glaciers, and extreme weather events threaten ecosystems and humanity.

Ignoring the problem is no longer an option; we need urgent action and sustainable solution

GHG emissions continue to rise despite efforts to reduce them.
