History of Pakistan

Pakistan was established on 14 August 1947 after a long struggle by Muslims of the Indian subcontinent. This struggle was lead by Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah.

British India was divided into two nations: India and Pakistan. The AIML played a major role in the foundation of Pakistan.

Important Events in Pakistan's History

  • 1940 - Lahore Resolution
  • 1947 - Independence
    • Partition of India
    • Migration of Riots
  • 1965 - First Constitution
  • 1971 - Seperation of East Pakistan
